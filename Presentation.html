Real-world loss stack — IEC 61724 / NREL PVWatts methodology
Losses compound multiplicatively — they do not simply add. The Performance Ratio (PR) is the single industry-standard metric summarising all losses. World average is 75–82%. Above 80% is excellent for Indian conditions.
PM Surya Ghar Yojana (2024): ₹30,000/kW for first 2 kW; ₹18,000/kW for the 3rd kW; capped at ₹78,000 above 3 kW. Additional state subsidies available in Gujarat, Rajasthan and UP. Net-metering lets you export surplus generation to the grid.
Sources: Grid CO₂ — CEA India 2023 (0.82 kg/kWh). Tree sequestration — FSI India (21 kg CO₂/tree/yr). Coal — 5.5 kWh/kg. Vehicles — MoRTH (4.6 t CO₂/car/yr).
